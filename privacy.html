Privacy Policy

Corgi Focus Club and its tool CFC Studio · Last updated: 26 October 2026

Who we are

Corgi Focus Club publishes cozy study-timer videos, Shorts and Pins. CFC Studio is our private desktop tool, used only by the owner of Corgi Focus Club to manage our own YouTube channel and Pinterest account. It is not distributed or offered to other users.

YouTube API Services

CFC Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using CFC Studio, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Your data is also handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

Pinterest API

CFC Studio uses the Pinterest API to read our own boards and create Pins of our own content on our own boards. Pinterest data is handled under the Pinterest Privacy Policy.

Data accessed

Only the owner's own accounts: YouTube channel information, video upload and management (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists, publish schedule, a pinned comment on our own videos), YouTube Analytics for our own channel, and our own Pinterest boards and Pins.

How it is used

Only to publish and schedule our own videos and Pins and to show our own channel statistics inside the app.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Statistics are shown in the app and kept for no longer than 30 days. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or sent to any other server. This website uses no cookies and no tracking.

Deleting your data and revoking access

You can revoke CFC Studio's access at any time via the Google security settings page or in your Pinterest settings under apps. Disconnecting in CFC Studio's Settings deletes the stored token and statistics from the computer. To request deletion of any stored data, email us; it will be deleted within 7 days.
