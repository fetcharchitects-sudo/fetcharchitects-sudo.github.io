CFC Studio

A private desktop tool for the Corgi Focus Club YouTube channel. It uploads and schedules the channel's own videos and shows channel statistics.

CFC Studio uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy

CFC Studio is used only by the owner of the Corgi Focus Club YouTube channel. It is not distributed or offered to other users.

YouTube API Services

CFC Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using CFC Studio, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Your data is also handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

Data accessed

YouTube account and channel information, video upload and management access (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists, publish schedule, a pinned comment on the owner's own videos), and YouTube Analytics for the owner's own channel only.

How it is used

Only to upload and schedule the owner's own videos, set thumbnails and playlists, and show channel statistics inside the app.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. Statistics are shown in the app and kept for no longer than 30 days. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or sent to any other server. No cookies or tracking are used.

Deleting your data and revoking access

You can revoke CFC Studio's access at any time via the Google security settings page. Disconnecting YouTube in CFC Studio's Settings deletes the stored token and statistics from the computer. To request deletion of any stored data, email the contact below; it will be deleted within 7 days.
