CFC Studio

A private desktop tool for the Corgi Focus Club YouTube channel. It uploads and schedules the channel's own videos and shows channel statistics.

Privacy Policy

CFC Studio is used only by the owner of the Corgi Focus Club YouTube channel.

Data accessed: YouTube account and channel data, video upload/management access, and YouTube Analytics for the owner's channel only.

How it is used: only to upload and schedule videos, set thumbnails and playlists, and show channel statistics inside the app.

Storage: sign-in tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or sent to any other server.

Revoking access: remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
